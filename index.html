The lore, such as it is

Triple-Vé Studio started as a sketchbook habit that got out of hand. No big publisher, no notes from a marketing team — just a small crew drawing the characters and stories they'd actually want to follow, then building games and animation around them. The signpost is our mark because that's what this whole thing is: word of mouth, one smoke trail at a time.

Why "Triple-Vé"? In French, the letter W is double-vé — literally "double V." We just kept going.
